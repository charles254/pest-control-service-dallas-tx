Frequently asked questions

Does a bed bug heat treatment damage my furniture, electronics, or clothing?

No. Controlled heat treatments maintain temperatures between 125°F and 135°F, which is well below the threshold that would harm electronics, appliances, wood furniture, or clothing. Only heat-sensitive items like candles, vinyl records, fresh produce, and medications must be removed.

How long does a residential bed bug heat treatment take in Dallas?

A typical whole-home thermal heat remediation takes approximately 6 to 8 hours from setup to completion. Homeowners and pets vacate the premises in the morning and return the same evening to a completely bed-bug-free home.

Can bed bugs travel between apartments in Dallas multi-family buildings?

Yes. Bed bugs readily migrate through electrical conduit channels, pipe chases, and common hallway baseboards. In multi-unit properties, inspecting adjoining units above, below, and on both sides of an infested unit is critical.

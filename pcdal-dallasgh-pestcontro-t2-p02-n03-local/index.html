Frequently asked questions

How does the two-step fire ant management method work?

Step one involves broadcasting a low-toxicity fire ant bait across your entire lawn in spring or fall when ants are actively foraging. Step two targets remaining nuisance mounds near high-traffic patio zones with precision liquid drenches or specialized mound treatments.

Are striped bark scorpion stings dangerous to humans in Dallas?

Striped bark scorpion stings are rarely life-threatening to healthy adults but cause intense localized pain, swelling, and numbness that can persist for 24 to 72 hours. Stings require immediate medical evaluation if severe allergic reactions, difficulty breathing, or anaphylaxis occur.

Why do scorpions glow under ultraviolet blacklight?

Scorpion exoskeletons contain beta-carboline and other fluorescent chemical compounds in their hard cuticle layer that absorb ultraviolet light and emit a bright, glowing blue-green color, making nighttime UV blacklight inspections highly effective.

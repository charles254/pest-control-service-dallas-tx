How quickly can an emergency exterminator reach my Dallas property?

Emergency technicians offer priority response across Dallas, Highland Park, University Park, Lake Highlands, and Preston Hollow, typically arriving on-site within 60 to 90 minutes for urgent stinging insect hazards or severe in-home pest breaches.

What is included in an official Texas WDI real estate inspection?

An official Texas WDI inspection includes a comprehensive visual and instrument-aided evaluation of all accessible interior and exterior structural components. The inspector documents active infestations, past damage, previous treatment evidence, and conducive conditions on the mandatory state-approved TDA/SPCS form.

Can attic rodents cause electrical house fires in Dallas homes?

Yes. Rodents must constantly gnaw hard materials to wear down their continuously growing incisor teeth. Roof rats frequently chew through thermoplastic electrical wire insulation in attic spaces, exposing energized copper wiring that can spark and ignite dry blown-in insulation.

Are termite treatments covered under standard Dallas homeowners insurance policies?

Standard homeowners insurance policies almost universally exclude gradual termite damage and ongoing pest treatments, classifying pest mitigation as regular property maintenance. Securing proactive warranty coverage through a licensed exterminator protects your equity.
